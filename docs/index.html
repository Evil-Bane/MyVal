Quick Answers

Will MyVal get my Valorant account banned?

No. MyVal runs exclusively on your Android phone and only reads your own official Riot companion data (the exact same info desktop tracker apps read). It never alters PC files, never injects overlays, and never gives an unfair in-game advantage.

Is my Riot login safe?

Yes. You sign in directly on Riot Games' official auth.riotgames.com login page. Your session stays encrypted on your own phone using Android Keystore security — it is never uploaded to any third-party backend.

How does 2D Match Replay work?

Tap any match in your Career or Last Match card to open the round-by-round 2D Replay. Scrub through rounds and kill timestamps to see exactly where every player was on the minimap when each kill, plant, or defuse happened.

How do I get future updates?

MyVal checks for new releases automatically and pops up an update prompt with a 1-tap download button right inside the app. You can also add https://github.com/Evil-Bane/MyVal in Obtainium for automatic background updates.
