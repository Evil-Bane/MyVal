Quick Answers

Can a user get banned for using MyVal?

Not happened to anyone we know. The developer also spoke directly with a Riot developer regarding companion apps — their verdict was that Riot generally does not mind third-party tools unless an automation, app, tool, or script gives a player any competitive advantage. MyVal never alters PC files, never injects overlays, and never automates gameplay; however, because it uses Riot companion endpoints rather than an official Riot Production API key, we never claim a 100% ban-safe guarantee.

Is my Riot login safe?

Yes. You sign in directly on Riot Games' official auth.riotgames.com login page. Your session stays encrypted on your own phone using Android Keystore security — it is never uploaded to any third-party backend.

How does 2D Match Replay work?

Tap any match in your Career or Last Match card to open the round-by-round 2D Replay. Scrub through rounds and kill timestamps to see exactly where every player was on the minimap when each kill, plant, or defuse happened.

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use (Zero Data Stored)

We store nothing on any server — zero analytics, zero trackers, and zero credential relays. Nobody forces login; you voluntarily authenticate directly inside Riot Games' official login page on your phone, keeping session tokens strictly inside Android Keystore. How do I get future updates?

MyVal checks for new releases automatically, downloads updates seamlessly inside the app, and opens Android's installer in 1 tap. You can also track https://github.com/Evil-Bane/MyVal in Obtainium.
